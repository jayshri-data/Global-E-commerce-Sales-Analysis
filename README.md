# Global E-commerce Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Global E-commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard** created using Microsoft Power BI.

The purpose of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data from **2023 to 2025** and understand overall business performance through sales, profit, orders, products, payment methods, discounts, categories, and geographical analysis.
I created this dashboard to turn raw sales data into meaningful and easy-to-understand insights that can help in making better business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:
* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Track total orders and profit margin
* Identify the top-performing products
* Understand monthly sales trends
* Compare profit across product categories
* Analyze sales by payment method
* Understand the relationship between discounts and profit
* Analyze sales performance across regions and countries
* Provide an interactive dashboard for business users

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis provided several useful insights from the available data:

* The business generated approximately **510.36K in total sales**.
* Total profit was approximately **158.87K**.
* The overall **profit margin was 31.1%**.
* The **Furniture** category generated the highest profit among the categories shown in the dashboard.
* The **Standing Desk Converter** was one of the highest-selling products in the Top 10 Products analysis.
* Monthly sales changed throughout the year, with **October recording the highest sales** in the displayed monthly trend.
* Credit Card, PayPal, Cash on Delivery, and Bank Transfer were analyzed to understand customer payment preferences.
* The discount analysis showed that the **Low Discount** group generated the highest profit.
* The geographical view helps compare sales performance across different countries and regions.
* The detailed order table provides a more granular view of individual orders, customers, products, sales, profit, and quantity.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. **Focus on high-performing products**
   Continue promoting products that generate strong sales and profitability.

2. **Monitor discount strategies**
   Discounts should be planned carefully because higher discounts can affect profitability.

3. **Improve regional performance**
   Analyze regions with lower sales and identify opportunities to increase customer engagement.

4. **Focus on profitable categories**
   Give more attention to product categories that contribute strongly to overall profit.

5. **Use sales trends for planning**
   Monthly sales trends can help with inventory, marketing, and sales planning.

6. **Understand customer payment preferences**
   Payment method analysis can help businesses provide convenient payment options for customers.
